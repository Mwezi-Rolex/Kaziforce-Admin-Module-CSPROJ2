## Figma screens and components with no SRS requirement

No whole Figma screen is unmapped. These components are backed by Chapter 5 Update or Note 8 rather than by an SRS statement:

- `dashboard` KPI panel: intentionally unpopulated; no KPI requirement exists in the SRS or Chapter 5.
- `queue` escalation-review lane with overrule and uphold-and-ban actions: from the Note 8 activity diagram and the Chapter 5 §3.6.4 workflow; the SRS has no moderation-queue requirement.
- `queue` bulk approve, reject and escalate controls: required by Chapter 5 §3.6.4; not specified in the SRS.
- `ConfirmDialog` two-step destructive confirmation: a UX choice supporting the audit and change-control intent, not an SRS ID.
- `settings` key/value and feature-toggle rows: the interfaces are required by FR-ADM-019 and FR-ADM-026, but no key or toggle names are specified in the SRS or Chapter 5.
- `login` session-refresh and role-claim wording on the sign-in card: the JWT mechanics come from FR-IAM-021 and FR-IAM-022, but the copy is a Figma decision.
